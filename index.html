Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Banerise ("we", "our", "us") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with our application(s) that integrate with the Amazon Advertising API and related Amazon Selling Partner services, used to manage advertising campaigns for our Amazon store.

1. Who We Are

Banerise is an e-commerce brand operating on Amazon Japan. Our application connects to Amazon's Advertising API solely to manage and analyze our own advertising campaigns, keyword performance, and related account data.

2. Information We Collect

  • Advertising campaign data obtained via the Amazon Advertising API (campaign structure, keywords, bids, performance metrics such as impressions, clicks, spend, and sales).
  • Account and authorization information (such as access tokens) required to connect to the Amazon Advertising API.
  • Basic technical information (such as log data) generated when our application communicates with Amazon's API.

We do not collect sensitive personal information from Amazon buyers. Our application does not access or process end-customer personally identifiable information (PII) beyond what Amazon's Advertising API itself provides for campaign management purposes.

3. How We Use Information

  • To manage, optimize, and report on advertising campaigns for our own Amazon store.
  • To analyze keyword and search term performance in order to improve advertising efficiency.
  • To maintain the security and proper functioning of our application.

4. Data Storage and Security

Data obtained through the Amazon Advertising API is stored securely and access is restricted to authorized personnel only. We apply reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect data from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent advertising data obtained via the Amazon Advertising API to third parties. Data may only be shared with service providers strictly necessary to operate our application, or as required by law.

6. Data Retention

We retain advertising data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, or as required by Amazon's API terms and applicable law.

7. Your Rights

If you have questions about the data we process or wish to make a request regarding your information, please contact us using the details below.
